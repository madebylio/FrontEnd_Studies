ABOUT ME

Building stories through code, photography and creativity.

Hi, I'm Gustavo, also known as Liobor. I'm a creative developer and photographer passionate about transforming ideas into visual experiences.

My journey started through photography, where I learned composition, storytelling and attention to detail. Over time, this curiosity expanded into design, video editing and web development.

Currently I'm studying frontend development, JavaScript and modern web technologies while building personal projects and exploring creative coding.

Beyond technology, I enjoy visual arts, photography, digital experiences and creating projects that connect aesthetics and functionality.
